Privacy policy

The tool accesses Google Drive files and file metadata only to synchronize files selected by the operator. It runs on the operator’s computer and stores Google authorization locally. Files are transferred only between that computer and the operator’s Google Drive account.

The tool does not collect data from other users, share Google user data with third parties, or use it for advertising or analytics. The operator can revoke access in their Google Account and remove the local authorization.
